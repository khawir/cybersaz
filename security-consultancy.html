Services · Consultancy

Security Consultancy

Cyber security consultancy services at CybrixAZ offer comprehensive support led by a team of experts to design, implement and optimize security measures based on each client's specific needs — providing the best solutions and expert opinion, based on industry standards, to protect digital assets and fulfil regulatory requirements.

Foundation

Security Design

Our Security Design services focus on creating a strong foundation for your security infrastructure. This involves identifying gaps in your current security posture, meeting client expectations, and providing the best-fit solutions for your existing security infrastructure — including network design, data protection strategies, access control measures and more.

Technology

Security Solutions

CybrixAZ offers consultancy services to evaluate customer requirements and define security solutions that address each client's needs and challenges — including recommending and implementing advanced security technologies such as SIEM, SOAR, DLP, PAM, IAM, IGA, EDR/XDR, NDR, MFA, ZTNA and database security. We ensure these solutions are seamlessly integrated into your existing IT environment to provide maximum protection with minimal disruption.

Deployment

Security Implementation

Security Implementation services ensure the effective deployment, implementation and testing of security solutions, considering both the client's requirements and industry best-practice standards. The aim is to provide end-to-end implementation support, including configuration, testing and validation of security technologies. Our team works closely with your security and IT staff to ensure a smooth transition and to train them on the new solutions.

Development Lifecycle

DevSecOps Implementation

DevSecOps services ensure effective, seamless integration with the software development lifecycle to identify and mitigate security risks before they manifest in production. CybrixAZ provides guidance on secure coding practices, continuous security testing, vulnerability management and automated security checks, so client applications stay secure from development through to deployment.

Continuous Improvement

Security Optimization

Security Optimization services focus on enhancing the performance and effectiveness of a client's existing security solutions and policies. Our team helps clients conduct regular assessments and audits to identify areas for improvement, and provides recommendations to optimize the security infrastructure. The goal is to ensure that security measures across your IT infrastructure stay up to date and capable of defending against emerging threats.
