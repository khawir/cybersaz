Security Assessments

As part of a pre- and post-deployment approach to cyber security, security assessments play a vital role in enhancing and optimizing cyber security controls and configurations. We offer assessment services in line with industry best practices and standards, including ISO 27000, NIST CSF and 800-53, CIS, HIPAA, HITRUST, SOC 2 and GDPR.

SOC

SOC Maturity Assessment

The purpose of the SOC Maturity Assessment is to assess the current state of a Security Operations Center and identify gaps between its current and desired maturity and capability. This exercise provides precise recommendations and milestones to achieve the desired state, performed based on the Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI), NIST CSF, ISO 27001 and industry best practices.

It evaluates and improves the effectiveness, efficiency and readiness of the SOC in defending against cyber security threats and responding to security incidents — providing a comprehensive understanding of the SOC's current state and its alignment with industry best practices and organizational goals.

Organization-wide

Cyber Security Maturity Assessment

A comprehensive review of the organization's cyber security posture, assessing the effectiveness of security controls, policies and procedures. It quantifies your current posture and provides actionable steps to achieve a desired one. The model consists of five levels or stages representing the degree of sophistication and effectiveness of your cyber security processes, controls and outcomes.

This assessment helps organizations understand their current cyber security maturity level and provides recommendations aligned with industry best practices.

Industrial

OT Security Maturity Assessment

Provides organizations with a clear understanding of their current OT (Operational Technology) security posture, identifies areas for improvement, and develops a roadmap to enhance resilience against cyber threats targeting industrial control systems and critical infrastructure. Performed based on the Cybersecurity Capability Maturity Model (C2M2) and NIST CSF.

Uses a set of industry-vetted cyber security practices focused on both information technology (IT) and operational technology (OT) assets and environments.

Development Lifecycle

DevSecOps Maturity Assessment

Evaluates the integration of security practices within the DevOps pipeline, examining the maturity of security measures throughout the software development lifecycle to ensure that security is embedded in development, deployment and operations processes.

Performed based on four maturity models to evaluate the current state of security measures from development, testing and integration through to deployment, with actions to achieve the desired maturity level.

Investigative

Compromise Assessment

A thorough investigation to determine whether an organization's systems and networks have been compromised by malicious actors — examining logs, system artefacts and network traffic to identify indicators of compromise and provide recommendations for remediation.
