How we work

We start every engagement by understanding the client's environment, risk appetite and regulatory obligations before recommending a solution — not the other way around. That's why our services span the full lifecycle: design and consultancy up front, hands-on implementation and testing in the middle, and managed operations and continuous assessment afterward, so security holds up after the engagement ends.

Our assessments and audits are built against recognized frameworks — including ISO 27000, NIST CSF and 800-53, CIS, HIPAA, HITRUST, SOC 2 and GDPR — so the results map cleanly onto whatever standard your organization is accountable to.
