Managed SOC

Our Managed Security Operations Centre (SOC) offers comprehensive, around-the-clock monitoring, threat detection and response services to safeguard our clients' assets. Our SOC team leverages best practices to ensure your organization's data remains secure. The following services are available within our Managed SOC.

Tier 1

SOC L1 Services

  • Continuous Monitoring (24/7): continuous monitoring of security events and alerts using SIEM systems.
  • Initial Incident Triage: triage of security incidents to determine severity and urgency.
  • Basic Threat Analysis: analysis of security events to identify potential threats and vulnerabilities.
Tier 2

SOC L2 Services

  • In-depth Incident Analysis: detailed analysis of security incidents to understand root cause and impact.
  • Incident Response Coordination: coordination with stakeholders to ensure timely, effective response.
  • Threat Containment and Mitigation: containment and mitigation strategies to minimize impact.
Tier 3

SOC L3 Services

  • Advanced Threat Analysis: advanced threat analysis and digital forensics to identify root causes and threat actors.
  • Proactive Threat Hunting: proactive threat hunting to identify and mitigate potential threats before they materialize.
  • Threat Intelligence: gathering threat intelligence feeds and solution enrichment for proactive defence.
  • Security Advisory: expert advisory services to help strengthen overall security posture.
  • Threat Actor Profiling: profiling threat actors to understand tactics, techniques and procedures (TTPs).

Compromise Assessments

Through proactive compromise assessment we evaluate your network and help identify signs of malicious activity that may have gone undetected. A compromise assessment gives you confidence that your IT environment is secure — we provide actionable recommendations to enhance your security posture, helping you address vulnerabilities and implement measures to prevent future compromises.

Threat Hunting

Through threat hunting services we actively and continuously assess and identify signs of malicious activity and zero-days within your network. Our Threat Hunting services are designed to identify and neutralize advanced threats that may evade traditional security measures.
